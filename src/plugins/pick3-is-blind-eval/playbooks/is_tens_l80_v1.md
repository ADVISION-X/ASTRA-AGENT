# IS Stream B L80 Playbook v1

Position: `stream_b`

Stream B rule:

- Judge Stream B independently.
- Require confirmation before following one-stroke impulse.
- Treat shoulder-area moves with extra caution.

Lower-leaning cues:

- Low or middle tail fails to hold above the shoulder after a rebound.
- High tail returns toward the shoulder without renewed high support.
- Tail shows shoulder rejection from below or near 4.5.

Upper-leaning cues:

- Two or more recent points hold above the shoulder after crossing.
- Low extreme rebounds with continuation rather than a single isolated pop.
- High platform remains supported after a shallow dip.
- `shoulder_hold` is the strongest Stream B upper signal; `oscillation_hold_upper` is valid only when the upper return repeats after dips.

Abstain cues:

- Low rebound is strong but not confirmed above 4.5.
- High shallow dip stops near 4.5 without clear continuation.
- Tail chop makes both lower and upper plausible.

Confidence rule:

- Use 0.51-0.60 for `lower` or `upper`. Follow the calibration scale in visual-grammar.md.
- Use null confidence with `abstain`.
