# Pick3 IS L80 Visual Grammar

Use this grammar to describe the unfinished tail of one no-text chart.

Motion words:

- `surge_up`: large upward tail stroke.
- `rise`: clear upward move.
- `slow_rise`: small upward move.
- `hold`: flat or nearly flat tail.
- `slow_fall`: small downward move.
- `fall_back`: clear downward move from a higher area.
- `kill_down`: large downward drop.

Shape words:

- `sharp_top`: upward motion followed by downward pressure.
- `sharp_bottom`: downward motion followed by upward pressure.
- `upward_inertia`: repeated upward tail movement.
- `downward_inertia`: repeated downward tail movement.
- `shoulder_return`: tail moves back toward the 4.5 line.
- `shoulder_reject`: tail approaches 4.5 but fails to cross or hold.
- `high_pressure`: repeated upper-band contact above the shoulder.
- `low_pressure`: repeated lower-band contact below the shoulder.
- `disordered_chop`: alternating moves with no stable continuation.

Decision discipline:

- Interpret the last 3-8 points first, then check the broader L80 context.
- Shoulder line 4.5 is a reference axis, not an automatic crossing rule.
- Momentum alone is not enough.
- One extreme stroke often creates reversal risk.
- Noisy contradiction means `abstain`.
