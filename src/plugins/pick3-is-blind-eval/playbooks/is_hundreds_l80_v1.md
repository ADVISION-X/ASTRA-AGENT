# IS Stream A L80 Playbook v1

Position: `stream_a`

Stream A rule:

- Judge Stream A independently.
- Do not borrow Stream B or Stream C behavior.
- Prefer clear tail structure over broad historical feel.

Lower-leaning cues:

- High tail extends far above the shoulder and then bends down toward the shoulder.
- Tail rises toward 4.5 from below but does not clearly hold above it.
- High area shows a sharp top or repeated exhaustion.

Upper-leaning cues:

- Tail crosses the shoulder and holds above it for multiple recent points.
- Low extreme is followed by a clear rebound that continues through the shoulder.
- High area is not a single spike but a maintained platform.
- `shoulder_hold` and `oscillation_hold_upper` are the strongest Stream A upper signals when they persist across the tail.

Abstain cues:

- Last strokes alternate hard up/down without a stable read.
- Tail sits near the shoulder with no hold/reject evidence.
- One extreme move conflicts with the previous 3-8 point structure.

Confidence rule:

- Use 0.51-0.60 for `lower` or `upper`. Follow the calibration scale in visual-grammar.md.
- Use null confidence with `abstain`.
