# Pick3 IS Ones L80 Playbook v1

Position: `ones`

Ones stream rule:

- Judge ones independently.
- Be stricter than hundreds when the tail is impulsive.
- Do not over-trust low crash continuation or high spike reversal.

Front-leaning cues:

- Tail approaches the shoulder from below and visibly rejects.
- High spike is isolated and followed by shoulder-return pressure.
- Recent high area loses support across multiple points.

Back-leaning cues:

- Low crash rebounds and then holds above the shoulder.
- High platform persists instead of forming a single isolated spike.
- Tail leaves low pressure with confirmed upward structure.

Abstain cues:

- One-stroke crash or one-stroke spike dominates the tail.
- Consecutive moves approach 4.5 but do not prove hold or reject.
- Recent points are disordered and visually contradictory.

Confidence rule:

- Use 0.51-0.60 for `front` or `back`.
- Use null confidence with `abstain`.
